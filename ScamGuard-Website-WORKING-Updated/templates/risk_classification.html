Understanding Risk Levels

ScamGuard uses risk levels to communicate how suspicious a message or URL may be.

🟢 LOW (0-25)

Few or no suspicious indicators are present. The content appears relatively safe, but users should still remain cautious.

🟡 MEDIUM(26-50)

Some suspicious indicators are present. Users should verify the sender and information before taking any action.

🟠 HIGH(51-75)

Strong scam indicators are present. Users should avoid clicking links, sharing information or making payments.

🔴 CRITICAL(76-100)

Multiple serious scam indicators are present. The user should stop interaction immediately and verify the source through an official channel.

How Risk Helps Users

Risk classification helps users understand the seriousness of suspicious content and decide what action should be taken.
