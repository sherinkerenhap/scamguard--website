Stay Safe From Digital Scams

When a message or URL appears suspicious, follow these safety recommendations.

🔐 Never Share OTPs

Never share OTPs, verification codes, passwords or PINs with another person.

🔗 Avoid Suspicious Links

Do not click unexpected links received through SMS, email or social media.

💳 Verify Payment Requests

Verify unexpected payment or money transfer requests using an official source.

📱 Verify the Sender

Do not trust a message only because it appears to come from a familiar organization.

⚠️ Do Not Act Under Pressure

Scammers often create urgency or fear. Take time to verify the information.

🏦 Contact Official Sources

If you receive a suspicious banking or account message, contact the organization through its official website or customer support.

🛡️ Official Cyber Scam Reporting

Report suspicious messages or impersonation calls through the official Sanchar Saathi – Chakshu reporting portal.
